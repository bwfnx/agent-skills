# Follow-up menu guidance

After the draft email, present a **Follow-up Menu** as a **table**.

## Requirements
- Assign a letter to each option (A, B, C...).
- Automatically tailor options to what happened in the meeting.
- Always include:
  - **A**: "Map this directly to Nexus/NeoSerra milestone and counseling dropdown selections and auto-generate the exact NeoSerra dropdown selections"
    - If you do not have the exact dropdown taxonomy, provide best-effort guesses and explicitly label them as "best-effort".
    - If the user provides a taxonomy later, update the mapping and regenerate.
  - At least one option explicitly labeled as **"high value, minimum effort"**.

## Suggested columns
- Option
- Deliverable
- Why it helps
- Inputs needed from consultant/client
- Effort (S/M/L)

## Suggested option ideas (mix + match)
- Project management 30-day sprint + gantt-style plan
- Market and industry analysis + competitive review
- Grant eligibility triage + search shortlist
- Funding readiness checklist (financials, pitch, narrative)
- Government contracting pipeline + certifications roadmap
- Digital presence quick wins (website, SEO basics, listings)
- Sales outreach sequence + CRM tracking approach
- Capability statement review and rewrite
- KPI dashboard (simple spreadsheet format)
