What comes back: the follow-up email

SBDC Next Steps — Harbor Lane Bakery

Hi Dana,

It was nice speaking with you today. Below are my notes, along with some helpful documents and links.

Next Steps

  1. Complete a one-page business plan covering the second deck oven and walk-in cooler (~$45,000 installed) and its payback timeline. I'll send our template.
  2. Starting this month, split wholesale and retail revenue and costs in your bookkeeping so a lender can see both sides.
  3. Hold off on a fourth wholesale account until the new equipment is in. Write down what it would order and cost to produce.
  4. Before going back to your bank, we'll compare an SBA 7(a) against an SBA microloan for the $45,000.
  5. For our next session, bring two years of tax returns and your split books.

Notes

  • One-page plan checklist: itemized equipment cost, monthly revenue by season, payback calculation.
